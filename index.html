這是什麼 App

Tixory 幫你記錄每一場演唱會、音樂祭、音樂劇、舞台劇等活動：日期、場館、座位、進出場時間、交通、歌單、照片與備註，並用票根卡片、行事曆和年度回顧呈現。所有資料只儲存在你的裝置上。

常見問題

怎麼從售票網站帶入活動？

在售票網站的活動介紹頁按「分享 → 拷貝」，到 Tixory 新增活動的「從售票網址帶入」貼上，按「帶入這頁資訊」，名稱、日期、場館和封面會自動填好。讀不到的欄位可以再自己補上。

為什麼有些網址帶入失敗？

請確認貼上的是活動介紹頁，而不是購票、排隊或會員頁面。需要登入才能看的頁面，或網站改版時，可能會讀不到資訊，這時可以手動填寫。

怎麼記錄進場、出場時間？

在活動當天打開活動頁，按「我進場了」「我出場了」就會記下當下時間；之後也可以在「編輯」裡修改。

可以設定提醒嗎？

可以。在活動中設定提醒時間，Tixory 會在開演前用本機通知提醒你。第一次使用時需要允許通知。

我的資料會上傳到雲端嗎？

不會。所有活動、照片與備註都只存在你自己裝置的本機儲存空間，App 沒有自己的伺服器，開發者無法存取你的內容。目前也還沒有雲端同步，換手機前請留意。

怎麼刪除活動？

在活動列表長按活動，選擇「刪除」。刪除後無法復原。

Tixory — Concert Ticket Diary

Support (English)

About

Tixory helps you keep a record of every concert, festival, musical or play you attend: date, venue, seat, entry and exit times, transit, setlist, photos and notes, shown as ticket-stub cards, a calendar and a yearly recap. All data is stored only on your device.

Need help?

For questions, feedback, or bug reports, email jaceappstudio@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

FAQ

How do I import an event from a ticketing site?

On the event page of the ticketing site, tap Share → Copy. In Tixory, paste the link into "import from ticket URL" when adding an event, and the title, date, venue and cover are filled in for you. Anything that can't be read can be filled in by hand.

Why does importing fail for some links?

Make sure the link is the event's information page, not a checkout, queue or member page. Pages that require signing in, or sites that have changed their layout, may not be readable.

Is my data uploaded to the cloud?

No. Your events, photos and notes are stored only on your device. The app has no server of its own, and the developer cannot access your content. Cloud sync is not available yet.

How do I delete an event?

Long-press the event in the list and choose Delete. Deleted events cannot be recovered.
