Tixory 演唱會票根本 — 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-11-12

Tixory（以下稱「本 App」）重視你的隱私。本 App 不會蒐集、儲存或分享你的任何個人資料。

資料的儲存方式

  • 你記錄的活動（名稱、日期、場館、座位、進出場時間、交通、歌單、票券與消費金額、備註）以及加入的照片，都只儲存在你自己裝置的本機儲存空間中。
  • 本 App 沒有自己的後端伺服器，不需要註冊帳號，也沒有串接任何分析工具或第三方廣告服務。開發者無法存取你的內容。

對外連線

只有在你使用「從售票網址帶入」時，本 App 才會由你的裝置直接連到你貼上的售票網頁，讀取公開的活動名稱、日期、場館與海報圖；載入範例資料時也會下載範例活動的海報圖。這些連線不經過我們的伺服器，相關資料處理適用各網站自己的隱私權政策。點選歌單中的歌曲或「前往售票頁」時，會開啟對應的音樂 App 或網站。

權限

  • 照片：加入封面、座位視野圖或活動照片時，會開啟系統的照片選擇器，本 App 只會取得你選擇的照片。儲存票根圖時，會請求「加入照片」的權限，只用於把圖片存進你的相簿。
  • 通知：僅用於你設定的活動提醒，提醒在裝置上排程，不經過任何伺服器。
  • 行事曆：「加入行事曆」使用系統的新增事件畫面，由你確認後才會寫入，本 App 不會讀取你的行事曆。

本 App 不會索取通訊錄、定位等與功能無關的權限。

資料的刪除

你可以隨時在 App 中刪除活動，刪除後無法復原。若你刪除 App，所有本機儲存的資料將一併移除，且無法復原。

兒童隱私

本 App 不會刻意蒐集任何人的個人資料，包括兒童。

政策更新

若本政策有變更，會更新於此頁面並修改上方的日期。

Tixory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Tixory ("the app") respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store or share any personal data.

How your data is stored

  • The events you record (title, date, venue, seat, entry and exit times, transit, setlist, tickets and prices, notes) and the photos you add are stored only in local storage on your device.
  • The app has no backend server of its own, requires no account, and uses no analytics or third-party ad services. The developer cannot access your content.

Network connections

Only when you use "import from ticket URL" does the app connect directly from your device to the ticketing page you pasted, to read the public event title, date, venue and poster image. Loading the sample data also downloads poster images for the sample events. These connections do not go through our servers, and each website's own privacy policy applies. Tapping a song in a setlist or "open ticket page" opens the corresponding music app or website.

Permissions

  • Photos: the system photo picker opens when you add a cover, seat view or event photos, and the app receives only the photos you select. Saving a ticket-stub image asks for add-only photo access, used only to save that image to your library.
  • Notifications: used only for event reminders you set. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Calendar: "Add to Calendar" uses the system's new-event screen; nothing is written until you confirm, and the app never reads your calendar.

The app does not request contacts, location, or other permissions unrelated to its features.

Deleting your data

You can delete events in the app at any time; deleted events cannot be recovered. Deleting the app permanently removes all local data.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
